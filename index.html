How It Works

Simple steps to recover your lost belongings

1. Search & Browse

Use our search tools to look through all found items. Filter by category, date found, or location to quickly find what you're looking for.

2. Submit Claim

Found your item? Submit a claim with details about when and where you lost it. We'll verify the information and contact you.

3. Get Your Item Back

Once verified, coordinate pickup with our staff. Most items are returned within 24-48 hours of claim approval.
